About us

MADE FOR THE STREETS.
BUILT TO LAST.

Urban Threads is a Manila-born streetwear label making quality basics that young people can actually afford.

Our story

From a dorm room to your closet

Urban Threads started in 2021 when two IT students printed their first batch of 50 tees in a small dorm room in Quezon City. They wanted clothes that felt premium without the premium price tag.

Today we design every piece in-house and work with local manufacturers who share our focus on quality fabric, honest pricing and fair work.

Our Mission

To make well-made, trend-forward streetwear accessible to students and young professionals across the Philippines.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted local streetwear brand — known for quality, community and confident everyday style.
